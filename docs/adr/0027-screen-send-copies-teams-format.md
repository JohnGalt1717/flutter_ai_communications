# Screen send copies Teams capture size and VBSS format

Capture is the source’s physical pixels: a display is that display, a window is that window (not padded to the monitor), All-displays is the stitched bounding viewport. Teams content then sends at most 1920×1080, default low fps for documents, and Optimize raises fps (resolution may drop). Requested Video Format for screen send is that raster capped at 1920×1080 keeping aspect ratio, 5 fps off / 30 fps on Screen motion. Native scale lives on the Production video path. This is not the camera 1280×720@30 default.
